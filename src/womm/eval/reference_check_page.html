What you are doing

For each new EU law, the Commission publishes an official report, the impact assessment, saying who the law affects and how. An AI tool turned those reports into an answer key, and our system is scored against it. No person has checked that key.

You check these items against the quoted report text, without seeing what the tool decided. If the tool agrees with you at least 9 times in 10, the key is trusted as it is; if not, the tool gets fixed before anything is scored with it.

The one question

Does this item belong in the answer key?

  • Keep: the highlighted text backs the claim: this law affects this group in this way. It is not a repeat of another item of the case.
  • Drop: the text doesn't back it; it's background, an option that was not chosen, a general remark, or says there is no real effect; or it repeats another item.
  • Not sure: you can't decide after reading carefully. Write a short note why.

Judge the substance, not the wording. Work alone: about 10 minutes in all. Keys: K keep, D drop, U not sure, N note, ← → move. Answers are saved in this browser as you go, so don't use a private window.
