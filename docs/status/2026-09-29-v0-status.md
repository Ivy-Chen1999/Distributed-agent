Dev noise measurement (case_02, 3 more runs, same version, LangSmith `womm-v0-baseline-9d71de66`):
coverage 0.43 / 0.43 / 0.57 (mean 0.48, stdev 0.08); omissions mean 0.44 (stdev 0.19).

**Finding:** run-to-run variance is large. The four case_02 runs produced 24, 16, 16 and 9
dossier impacts; fewer impacts meant lower coverage. c02_e02-e04 are always covered; e01
(sandbox guidance lowers legal-advice costs), e05 (investor access) and e06 (support measures do
not fully offset fixed costs) come and go. Likely causes: variable expert output volume and
synthesis over-merging. This is the first Failure Memory signal and matters for the v1 promotion
gate (R28): single runs are not a reliable baseline.
