Three claims the demo must not make

  • "The system improved" or "statistically shown", for any weak-mode or dev-mode decision.
  • "Human-verified" or "expert-validated" answer keys. The strongest honest wording: AI-drafted; a two-person sample check failed; corrected after that feedback by an AI full-text review, not re-checked by people.
  • "Drafts impact assessments" or "approaches official IAs". It recovers part of an IA's list of impacts from the proposal text, graded by its own model family.
